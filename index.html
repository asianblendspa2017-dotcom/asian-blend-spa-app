Booking & Cancellation Policy
  • Please arrive 10–15 minutes prior to your scheduled treatment.
  • First-time guests will be required to complete a pre-consultation form.
  • Guests younger than 16 must be accompanied by an adult for the duration of treatment.
  • Please switch off cellphones during treatment.
  • Book at least 24 hours ahead, except on holidays and Sundays.
  • Home services require 48 hours notice, a minimum of 3 people, minimum 90-minute session, 50% deposit, and a transport fee per kilometer.
  • Full payment is required to secure weekend couples massage bookings and group bookings.
  • Cancellations made less than 24 hours before your appointment, or no-shows, will be charged the full treatment fee.
  • Late arrivals may result in a shortened session, with the full fee still applying.
  • All prices are subject to change without prior notice.
