Our specialty · Philippine Hilot

The healing hands of the Philippines, here in Cape Town.

Long before spas, every Filipino village had its manghihilot, a traditional healer whose skill was handed down from elder to apprentice within families for generations. Hilot comes from the Tagalog word for rubbing and pressing. It is an intuitive, hands-on art that combines massage, gentle joint alignment and energy work to bring the body back into balance.

As Cape Town's first Filipino massage clinic, Hilot is the heart of Asian Blend Spa. Every session is personal: your therapist reads your body first, then works where you actually need it, not to a fixed routine.

The Hilot ritual: four traditional steps

01

Pakiramdam

Feeling the body

Before any pressure, your therapist reads the body with open palms, finding where tension and lamig (trapped cold) have settled.

02

Dahon ng saging

Warm banana leaf

Warmed banana leaves are laid across the back. Traditionally, the leaf dries fastest where heat and tension gather, showing where the work is needed.

03

Langis ng niyog

Warm coconut oil

Warm virgin coconut oil, long rhythmic palm strokes, and thumb and forearm pressure release knots, ease the joints and restore energy flow.

04

Pahinga

Rest & restore

A slow, unhurried close with gentle stretching, so your nervous system settles before you step back into your day.

Contraindications & safety

Asian bodywork is generally very safe for healthy people. These are the situations where we change the plan, shorten it, or ask you to see a doctor first.

When we stop and ask for clearance

    Universal rules for every guest

    • Tell your therapist about every condition, medication, recent injury, surgery or pregnancy — every single visit.
    • Pain is a stop signal, not a target. "Good discomfort" is warm and broad; sharp, electric or nauseating is not.
    • No deep work over bruises, swelling, varicose veins, open skin or a numb area.
    • No strong abdominal work, no deep spinal manipulation, no forcing a joint.
    • Stop immediately if you feel dizzy, faint, short of breath, or have chest pain — and seek medical help.
    • Drink water afterwards. Warm oil and heat can drop your blood pressure, so rise slowly.
    This app is wellness education, not healthcare. Nothing here diagnoses, treats or cures any condition. Asian Blend Spa therapists are bodywork practitioners, not doctors. Always follow your own medical team's advice over anything in this plan.
